1. Who we are

Debt Relief Advantage operates as a lead-generation and referral service. We connect consumers who are interested in debt relief with third-party debt relief, debt settlement, and financial service providers and their marketing partners. We are not a lender, law firm, credit repair organization, or debt settlement company, and we do not provide legal, tax, or financial advice.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

When you complete a form or contact us, we may collect your first and last name, telephone number, email address, state of residence, estimated debt amount, income range, and any other information you choose to provide.

b. Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages viewed, referring URL, and similar usage data through cookies, pixels, and comparable technologies.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and provide the services you asked for;
  • To match you with, and share your information with, debt relief and financial service providers and marketing partners that may be able to assist you;
  • To contact you by phone, text message, email, or mail regarding debt relief and related offers, consistent with the consent you provide;
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4. How we share your information

We may share the information you submit with:

  • Debt relief and financial service providers and marketing partners, including JG Wentworth, so they may contact you about products or services that may fit your situation. A current list of categories of partners is available on our Marketing Partners page.

We do not sell your information for a fee to unrelated third parties for their own independent marketing outside of the referral relationships described above.
